    Featured project · 2026

    Ultra-Processed Food Scanner

    A photo of an ingredient list goes in; a NOVA group from 1 to 4 comes out, along with the ingredients the model read.

    What I built

    • A mobile food-scanning app in Python with PostgreSQL and Expo.
    • A vision-language model, Qwen3-VL via Ollama, reads the ingredient label.
    • Rule-based logic classifies the food into NOVA groups 1–4, with human review for uncertain scans.
    • A Streamlit web app: upload a photo, then see the NOVA classification and parsed ingredients. Validated with Python unit tests.

    Design choices

    • The display layer only turns the scanner's answer into a result card. It never decides a group itself.
    • Unsettled ingredients show "Likely ultra-processed, being verified" rather than a guess.
    • Unreadable photos, screenshots, cut-off lists and non-foods ask for another photo instead of showing a group.
    • Photos aren't saved and there's no account step. Unit tests run on sample output, without the model.
    Photo→Qwen3-VL (Ollama)→NOVA rules→Human review if uncertain→Result card

    Master's thesis · 06/2025 – 07/2026

    Topic Modeling Five Years of Crypto Discourse

    How did the conversation about cryptocurrency on YouTube shift over five years, and can topic models pick up shifts tied to real-world events?

    189,000+comments collected
    ~115,000comments in final corpus
    4topic-modeling methods compared
    5 yrsin six-month windows
    YouTube Data API→PostgreSQL→Preprocessing→4 topic models · RAPIDS cuML on GPU→Shifts over time

    What I did

    • Built a YouTube Data API pipeline that collected 189,000+ cryptocurrency comments, stored in PostgreSQL.
    • Retrieved and preprocessed the data into a corpus of ~115,000 comments.
    • Compared four topic-modeling methods across five years of discourse in six-month windows, with GPU acceleration (RAPIDS cuML).

    What it found

    • Detected topic shifts tied to real-world events, including the November 2022 FTX collapse.
    • …and the 2024 mining-cost debates.

    My role
